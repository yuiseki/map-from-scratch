# Day 02 — Lines

Show the paths of tropical cyclones worldwide from 1980 onward. Preserve each
storm as its own connected line, split paths at the antimeridian, and color the
tracks by IBTrACS basin. This is a map of recorded best-track positions; it does
not imply uniform observation intervals or uniform intensity measurements.

Data: IBTrACS v04r01 `since1980`, NOAA National Centers for Environmental
Information. NOAA/NCEI terms of use apply; cite the dataset and the 2010 BAMS
paper as requested by NCEI. This project snapshot was downloaded 2026-10-25.
