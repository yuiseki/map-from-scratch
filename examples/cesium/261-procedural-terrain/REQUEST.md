# cesium-261-procedural-terrain: 3D Procedural Fractal Terrain

Reproduce CesiumJS's showcase "Procedural Terrain" in pure WebGL2 without external dependencies.

## Key Features
- Dynamic procedural fractal mountain terrain generation:
  - Multi-octave ridged multifractal elevation with steep mountain crags and flat valley floors.
  - Analytical gradient normal computation for crisp mountain ridge lighting.
- Multi-biome elevation and slope-dependent texturing:
  - Deep blue water lakes with specular sun glint.
  - Golden sandy shorelines and transition beaches.
  - Lush valley forests and alpine meadows.
  - Rocky scree and granite cliff faces on steep slopes.
  - Glaciated snowcaps and white summits on high peaks.
- Atmospheric aerial distance fog and sky dome gradient.
- Interactive camera orbit navigation and adjustable water level and snow line sliders.
- Zero external runtime dependencies.
